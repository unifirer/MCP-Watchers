**Fault A.** `.grepai/config.yaml` had `embedder.endpoint: http://localhost:11434`.
Ollama on this machine is bound to **12134** (`OLLAMA_HOST=127.0.0.1:12134`, HKCU).

exits in **<2 s**, every time. Supervisor burned 5 restarts -> 10 min backoff -> repeat.

**Fault B (why "autoheal not working" is misleading).** The heal was **correctly
declining**. `Test-SupervisorAlive` saw a fresh `.sup` stamp (age 11 s vs 60 s window)
and took the documented single-healer branch: *"supervisor alive - skipping pane heal"*.
The supervisor it deferred to was mid-backoff. Both healers healthy; the thing being
healed could never start. `supervised restart pending` is truthful but reads like a stall.

## Premise correction (important)
The "11434 is in Windows reserved range 11408-11507" claim in
`Enable-GrepaiOllamaPortFix` (`###1...ps1` lines 659-733) is **FALSE here**.
`netsh int ipv4 show excludedportrange protocol=tcp` shows only 5357/5985/47001/50000-50059*.
11434 is not reserved. 11434 is empty simply because OLLAMA_HOST points at 12134.
